# Zanabûn Operator Theorems

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document establishes the principal theorems of the Zanabûn
Operator. The theorems formalize the epistemic guarantees provided by
the operator.

---

## Theorem Z-T1 — Epistemic Admissibility

If

ℤ(K) ≥ τz

then

K

is admissible for Mabûn processing.

---

## Theorem Z-T2 — Evidence Integrity

Every accepted knowledge state preserves its supporting evidence.

---

## Theorem Z-T3 — Explainability

Every accepted conclusion possesses at least one finite reasoning chain.

---

## Theorem Z-T4 — Consistency Preservation

If

Cons(K)=True

then

Cons(ℤ(K))=True

---

## Theorem Z-T5 — Hebûn Dependency

No valid Zanabûn evaluation exists for a state rejected by Hebûn.

---

## Theorem Z-T6 — Closure

If

K ∈ ΩH

then

ℤ(K) ∈ ΩH

---

## Theorem Z-T7 — Rasterast Eligibility

Every Zanabûn-valid state is admissible for Rasterast verification.

---

# Corollaries

## Corollary Z-C1

Every Mabûn evaluation implicitly assumes Hebûn and Zanabûn validity.

---

## Corollary Z-C2

Removing Zanabûn invalidates the canonical reasoning pipeline.
