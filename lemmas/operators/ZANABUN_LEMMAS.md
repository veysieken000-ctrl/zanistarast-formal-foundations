# Zanabûn Operator Lemmas

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document establishes the fundamental lemmas of the Zanabûn
Operator.

These lemmas follow from the formal definition, invariants and
propositions of the operator and prepare the basis for subsequent
theorems.

---

# Lemma ZL-1

## Deterministic Reasoning

For every Hebûn-valid state

Ψ

there exists a unique epistemic evaluation

ℤ(Ψ).

---

Consequence

Zanabûn is deterministic.

---

# Lemma ZL-2

## Evidence Preservation

If all premises are valid,

then every accepted conclusion preserves its supporting evidence.

---

# Lemma ZL-3

## Explainability

Every accepted reasoning chain can be reconstructed from its inference
steps.

---

# Lemma ZL-4

## Consistency Preservation

If the input state is epistemically consistent,

then Zanabûn preserves epistemic consistency.

---

# Lemma ZL-5

## Hebûn Dependency

No valid Zanabûn evaluation exists without a Hebûn-admissible input.

---

# Lemma ZL-6

## Composition Compatibility

Zanabûn composes with Mabûn, Rabûn and Rasterast while preserving the
Operator Specification.

---

# Lemma ZL-7

## Information Integrity

Zanabûn never increases epistemic inconsistency.
