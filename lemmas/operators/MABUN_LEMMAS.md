# Mabûn Operator Lemmas

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document establishes the first formal lemmas of the Mabûn
Operator.

The lemmas follow from the operator definition, invariants and
propositions.

---

# Lemma ML-1

## Deterministic Optimization

For every admissible resource state

R

there exists a unique optimized allocation

ℳ(R).

---

Consequence

Mabûn is deterministic.

---

# Lemma ML-2

## Feasible Allocation

Every accepted allocation belongs to the feasible region.

---

# Lemma ML-3

## Constraint Satisfaction

Optimization never violates declared constraints.

---

# Lemma ML-4

## Resource Conservation

For conserved quantities,

resource balance is preserved unless external inputs or outputs are
explicitly modeled.

---

# Lemma ML-5

## Equilibrium Improvement

If a feasible improvement exists,

Mabûn never decreases the equilibrium score.

---

# Lemma ML-6

## Hebûn–Zanabûn Dependency

Every Mabûn evaluation assumes both Hebûn-valid and Zanabûn-valid
states.

---

# Lemma ML-7

## Rasterast Compatibility

Every accepted optimization is eligible for Rasterast verification.
