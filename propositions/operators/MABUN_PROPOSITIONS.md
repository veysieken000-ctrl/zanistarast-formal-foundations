# Mabûn Operator Propositions

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document establishes the formal propositions of the Mabûn
Operator.

These propositions connect the optimization operator definition to the
subsequent lemmas and theorems.

---

## Proposition MP-1 — Well-Definedness

For every Zanabûn-valid state

Ψ ∈ ΩZ

the operator

ℳ(Ψ)

is well-defined.

---

## Proposition MP-2 — Closure

If

Ψ ∈ ΩZ

then

ℳ(Ψ) ∈ ΩZ

---

## Proposition MP-3 — Feasibility

Every accepted allocation belongs to the feasible solution set.

---

## Proposition MP-4 — Constraint Preservation

All optimization constraints remain satisfied after Mabûn evaluation.

---

## Proposition MP-5 — Resource Conservation

For conserved resources,

the total resource quantity remains invariant unless external sources or
sinks are explicitly modeled.

---

## Proposition MP-6 — Equilibrium Preservation

Every accepted optimization preserves or improves the equilibrium score.

---

## Proposition MP-7 — Dependency

No valid Mabûn evaluation exists without Hebûn and Zanabûn validity.

---

## Proposition MP-8 — Rasterast Compatibility

Every optimized state is admissible for Rasterast verification.
