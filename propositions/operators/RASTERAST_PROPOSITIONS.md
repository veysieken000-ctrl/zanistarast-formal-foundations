# Rasterast Operator Propositions

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document establishes the formal propositions of the Rasterast
Operator.

Rasterast verifies logical, semantic and proof consistency before a
state becomes globally admissible.

---

## Proposition RS-P1 — Well-Definedness

For every Rabûn-valid state

Ψ ∈ ΩR

the operator

ℛs(Ψ)

is well-defined.

---

## Proposition RS-P2 — Closure

If

Ψ ∈ ΩR

then

ℛs(Ψ) ∈ ΩV

---

## Proposition RS-P3 — Proof Validity

Every accepted state possesses at least one valid proof object.

---

## Proposition RS-P4 — Semantic Preservation

Verification preserves semantic correctness.

---

## Proposition RS-P5 — Traceability

Every verification step is reconstructible.

---

## Proposition RS-P6 — Dependency

No Rasterast evaluation exists without successful completion of

Hebûn

↓

Zanabûn

↓

Mabûn

↓

Rabûn.

---

## Proposition RS-P7 — Global Acceptance

Every Rasterast-valid state satisfies the global verification policy.
