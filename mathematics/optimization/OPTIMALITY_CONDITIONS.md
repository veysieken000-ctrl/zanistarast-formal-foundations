# Optimality Conditions

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the optimality conditions of the Zanistarast
Formal Foundations.

---

# Optimization Space

Optimization is performed over

ΩF

or one of its admissible subsets.

---

# Local Optimum

A state

Ψ*

is locally optimal if no admissible neighboring state improves the
selected objective.

---

# Global Optimum

A state

Ψ*

is globally optimal if it minimizes (or maximizes) the objective over
the entire feasible set.

---

# Pareto Optimum

For vector-valued objectives,

Ψ*

is Pareto optimal if no objective can be improved without degrading at
least one other objective.

---

# Verified Optimum

A verified optimum satisfies

Ψ* ∈ ΩV

and

Ψ*

is optimal under the selected optimization policy.

---

# Canonical Optimum

A canonical optimum satisfies

• All hard constraints

• Canonical operator ordering

• Rasterast verification

• Selected optimality criterion
