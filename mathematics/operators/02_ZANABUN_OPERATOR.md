Version 1.0

# Purpose

The Zanabûn Operator (ℤ) is the epistemic operator of the
Zanistarast Formal Foundations.

Its purpose is to evaluate whether information, knowledge and inference
are sufficiently reliable, coherent and justified to participate in
decision-making.

Every reasoning process that has passed Hebûn validation shall be
evaluated by Zanabûn before economic, governance or verification stages.
