# Operator Topology

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the topological structure of the Zanistarast
Operator Algebra.

The topology provides a mathematical framework for studying continuity,
convergence and neighborhoods of operators.

---

# Operator Space

Let

𝒪 = {ℋ, ℤ, ℳ, ℛb, ℛs}

denote the canonical operator set.

---

# Topological Space

Define

(𝒪, τ)

where

τ

is a topology on the operator set.

---

# Open Sets

Every open subset of τ represents a family of operators sharing common
structural properties.

---

# Neighborhoods

Every operator possesses a neighborhood consisting of operators with
similar admissibility and verification characteristics.

---

# Continuity

An operator transformation is continuous if nearby operators are mapped
to nearby operators under the chosen topology.

---

# Convergence

A sequence of operators converges if its limit preserves admissibility
and verification requirements.

---

# Compactness

Compact subsets correspond to finite admissible operator families.

---

# Connectedness

Connected operator spaces admit continuous transformations without
breaking admissibility.
