# Partial Operator Composition

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines when two Zanistarast operators may be legally
composed.

---

# Definition

Composition

∘

is a partial binary operation on the operator set.

Not every pair of operators admits composition.

---

# Admissibility Rule

Operator

F ∘ G

is defined iff

Codomain(G)=Domain(F)

---

# Canonical Admissible Compositions

ℤ ∘ ℋ

ℳ ∘ ℤ

ℛb ∘ ℳ

ℛs ∘ ℛb

---

# Non-Admissible Examples

ℋ ∘ ℛs

ℋ ∘ ℛb

ℤ ∘ ℛs

ℳ ∘ ℛs

---

# Canonical Pipeline

ℋ

↓

ℤ

↓

ℳ

↓

ℛb

↓

ℛs

---

# Properties

Composition preserves admissibility.

Composition preserves dependency order.

Composition never skips required operators.
