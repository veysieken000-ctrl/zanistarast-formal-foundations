# Zanistarast Meta-Axioms

## Purpose

Defines the principles that every formal axiom in Zanistarast must satisfy.

---

## Meta-Axiom MA1
Consistency

Every axiom must be internally consistent.

---

## Meta-Axiom MA2
Non-Contradiction

No axiom may contradict another accepted axiom unless the previous one is explicitly revised.

---

## Meta-Axiom MA3
Independence

Each primitive axiom should be independent whenever possible.

---

## Meta-Axiom MA4
Traceability

Every theorem must trace back to one or more axioms.

---

## Meta-Axiom MA5
Formalizability

Every axiom must be expressible in formal mathematical language.

---

## Meta-Axiom MA6
Computability

Whenever applicable, an axiom should admit computational implementation.

---

## Meta-Axiom MA7
Verifiability

An axiom should allow logical analysis, mathematical proof, simulation, or empirical evaluation depending on its scope.

---

## Meta-Axiom MA8
Extensibility

New axioms may extend the system without invalidating previously verified results.

---

## Meta-Axiom MA9
Version Control

Changes to axioms require explicit versioning and justification.

---

## Meta-Axiom MA10
Rasterast Compatibility

Every formal component must be compatible with the Rasterast verification layer.
