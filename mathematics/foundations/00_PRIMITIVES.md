# Zanistarast Primitive Concepts
Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the primitive (undefined) concepts of the
Zanistarast Formal Foundations.

Primitive concepts are accepted without formal definition and serve as
the basis for all subsequent definitions and axioms.

---

## Primitive P1

Entity

Notation

x

Interpretation

A distinguishable object within the modeled universe.

---

## Primitive P2

State

Notation

Ψ

Interpretation

The current condition of an entity.

---

## Primitive P3

Transformation

Notation

τ

Interpretation

A change from one state to another.

---

## Primitive P4

Relation

Notation

R

Interpretation

A connection between two or more entities.

---

## Primitive P5

Time

Notation

t

Interpretation

An ordering parameter for state evolution.

---

## Primitive P6

Observation

Notation

Obs

Interpretation

The process of obtaining information about a state.

---

## Primitive P7

Verification

Notation

Ver

Interpretation

The evaluation of whether a statement satisfies the accepted formal
rules.

---

## Primitive P8

Consistency

Notation

Cons

Interpretation

The absence of internal contradiction.

---

# Dependency

All definitions, axioms and theorems ultimately depend on these
primitive concepts.
