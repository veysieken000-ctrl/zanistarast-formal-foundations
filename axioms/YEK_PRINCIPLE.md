Yek Principle

Yek denotes the unique distinguishable identity of every created entity.

Every admissible entity possesses at least one invariant property that
distinguishes it from every other admissible entity.

This uniqueness is preserved throughout the canonical Zanistarast
pipeline.

Yek is a mathematical principle describing created uniqueness.

It is not identical with Ehad.

Ehad is exclusively the Divine attribute and is outside the mathematical
formalization.
