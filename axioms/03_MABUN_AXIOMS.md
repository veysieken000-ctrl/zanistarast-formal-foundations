# Mabûn Axioms
## Formal Economic and Resource Equilibrium

---

# Purpose

Mabûn defines the formal principles governing the distribution,
circulation, preservation and regeneration of resources inside a
complex adaptive system.

Unlike classical economics, Mabûn treats wealth, energy,
information and biological resources as conserved system flows.

---

# Definition M1

Let

R(t)

be the total resource vector of a civilization.

R consists of

• Energy
• Matter
• Knowledge
• Labor
• Capital
• Time

---

# Axiom M1
Conservation of Essential Resources

For every closed subsystem

ΔR ≥ −L

where

L = unavoidable natural losses.

No artificial mechanism may destroy essential resources faster than
their regenerative capacity.

---

# Axiom M2
Flow Principle

Resources must circulate.

Accumulation without circulation increases systemic entropy.

For every node i

Flow(i) > 0

must hold.

---

# Axiom M3
Equilibrium Principle

The resource distribution function

ρ(x)

must remain bounded.

There exists

ε > 0

such that

|ρ(i)-ρ(j)| < ε

for sustainable neighboring regions.

---

# Axiom M4
Regeneration Principle

Every extraction process

E

requires a regeneration process

G

G ≥ E

over long-term equilibrium.

---

# Axiom M5
Entropy Constraint

Economic entropy

S

must satisfy

dS/dt ≤ α

where α is the resilience threshold.

---

# Axiom M6
Human Priority Principle

Optimization objectives shall never maximize efficiency by
destroying fundamental human dignity.

Human Integrity

>

Pure Economic Utility

---

# Axiom M7
Adaptive Stability

A resilient economy continuously adapts.

Static equilibrium
is not sustainable.

Dynamic equilibrium
is required.

---
