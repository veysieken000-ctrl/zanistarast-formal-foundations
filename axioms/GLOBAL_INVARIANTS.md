# Global Invariants

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the global invariants preserved throughout the
Zanistarast Formal Foundations.

Unlike axioms, invariants are properties that remain true throughout
every admissible execution.

---

# I1 — Admissibility Invariant

Every canonical execution preserves admissibility.

---

# I2 — Consistency Invariant

Logical and structural consistency are preserved by every canonical
operator.

---

# I3 — Determinism Invariant

For identical admissible inputs,

the canonical execution produces identical outputs.

---

# I4 — Ordering Invariant

The canonical operator ordering

Hebûn

↓

Zanabûn

↓

Mabûn

↓

Rabûn

↓

Rasterast

cannot be violated.

---

# I5 — Verification Invariant

Once a state belongs to ΩV,

its verified status is preserved under every admissible operation.

---

# I6 — Safety Invariant

Canonical execution shall never intentionally produce an inadmissible
verified state.

---

# I7 — Constraint Invariant

All hard constraints remain satisfied during canonical execution.

---

# I8 — Traceability Invariant

Every admissible transition remains reconstructible.
