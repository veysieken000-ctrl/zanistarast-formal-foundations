# Hebûn Operator Invariants

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the quantities preserved by the Hebûn operator.

An invariant is a property that remains unchanged under the application
of the operator.

---

## Invariant I1 — Identity

The identity of an admissible entity is preserved.

Id(ℍ(Ψ)) = Id(Ψ)

---

## Invariant I2 — Ontological Membership

If

Ψ ∈ Ω

then

ℍ(Ψ) ∈ Ω

---

## Invariant I3 — Logical Consistency

A logically consistent state remains logically consistent after Hebûn
evaluation.

---

## Invariant I4 — Traceability

Every accepted state preserves its provenance.

---

## Invariant I5 — Structural Integrity

The structural organization of an accepted state is preserved.

---

## Invariant I6 — Verification Compatibility

Every Hebûn output remains eligible for Rasterast verification.
