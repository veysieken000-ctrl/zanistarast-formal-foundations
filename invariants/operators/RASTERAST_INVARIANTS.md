
# Rasterast Operator Invariants

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the invariants preserved by the Rasterast
Operator.

Rasterast guarantees that formal verification preserves logical,
semantic and structural correctness.

---

## Invariant VSI-1

Logical Soundness

Every accepted state satisfies all logical constraints.

---

## Invariant VSI-2

Semantic Consistency

Semantic meaning remains preserved throughout verification.

---

## Invariant VSI-3

Proof Integrity

Every verified state possesses at least one valid proof object.

---

## Invariant VSI-4

Traceability

The complete verification history remains reconstructible.

---

## Invariant VSI-5

Explainability

Every accepted verification result admits a finite explanation.

---

## Invariant VSI-7

Global Consistency

Verified states satisfy all global Zanistarast invariants.
