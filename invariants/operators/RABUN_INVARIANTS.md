# Rabûn Operator Invariants

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the formal invariants preserved by the Rabûn
Operator.

The invariants guarantee that coordination and governance actions do not
violate the fundamental properties established by Hebûn, Zanabûn and
Mabûn.

---

## Invariant RI-1

Policy Consistency

Every generated policy remains internally consistent.

---

## Invariant RI-2

Coordination Preservation

Distributed coordination shall preserve network connectivity whenever
required by the model.

---

## Invariant RI-3

Constraint Preservation

All global operational constraints remain satisfied.

---

## Invariant RI-4

Consensus Integrity

Consensus decisions remain valid under admissible coordination.

---

## Invariant RI-5

Hebûn Compatibility

Every coordinated state remains Hebûn-valid.

---

## Invariant RI-6

Zanabûn Compatibility

Every coordinated decision remains epistemically valid.

---

## Invariant RI-7

Mabûn Compatibility

Resource optimization remains preserved.

---

## Invariant RI-8

Rasterast Compatibility

Every Rabûn output is eligible for Rasterast verification.
