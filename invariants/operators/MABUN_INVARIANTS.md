# Mabûn Operator Invariants

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the quantities preserved by the Mabûn operator.

These invariants guarantee that optimization does not violate the
fundamental constraints of the Zanistarast framework.

---

## Invariant MI-1

Resource Conservation

The total quantity of conserved resources remains unchanged unless an
explicit external source or sink is modeled.

---

## Invariant MI-2

Feasibility

Every accepted allocation belongs to the feasible solution set.

---

## Invariant MI-3

Constraint Preservation

Operational constraints remain satisfied after optimization.

---

## Invariant MI-4

Balance

The equilibrium score shall not decrease under valid optimization.

---

## Invariant MI-5

Hebûn Compatibility

Every optimized state remains Hebûn-valid.

---

## Invariant MI-6

Zanabûn Compatibility

Every optimized state remains epistemically valid.

---

## Invariant MI-7

Rasterast Compatibility

Every optimized allocation is eligible for Rasterast verification.
