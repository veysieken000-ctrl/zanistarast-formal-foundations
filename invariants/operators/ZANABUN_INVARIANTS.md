# Zanabûn Operator Invariants

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the formal invariants preserved by the Zanabûn
Operator.

These invariants characterize the epistemic quantities that remain
unchanged under valid reasoning.

---

## Invariant ZI-1

Evidence Preservation

Every accepted conclusion must preserve the integrity of its supporting
evidence.

---

## Invariant ZI-2

Traceability

Every reasoning chain remains reconstructible.

---

## Invariant ZI-3

Consistency

Logical consistency cannot decrease after a valid Zanabûn evaluation.

---

## Invariant ZI-4

Explainability

Every accepted conclusion admits an explicit reasoning path.

---

## Invariant ZI-5

Knowledge Integrity

Validated knowledge cannot become internally contradictory without
external modification.

---

## Invariant ZI-6

Hebûn Compatibility

Every Zanabûn state remains Hebûn-valid.

---

## Invariant ZI-7

Rasterast Compatibility

Every Zanabûn output is eligible for Rasterast verification.
